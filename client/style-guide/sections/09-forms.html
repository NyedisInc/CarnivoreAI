form controls

A field is a hairline, not a frame. Labels are small tracked capitals above the control. Focus is the cyan 2px outline. Choices are native inputs (checkbox, radio, select) tinted with accent-color, so the platform keeps their keyboard and screen reader behaviour.

use
One line of free text: a name, a search, a filter. In Svelte, copy the .oc-field recipe: padding 7px 10px, 13px mono, 1px --oc-line-rule edge, 2px corners, --oc-ground-field; focus is the shared edge (focus.css): the edge itself a little brighter, same width, no outline. In a legacy dialog, call TextPromptModal.open({title, label, placeholder, defaultValue, confirmLabel, cancelLabel}), which replaces window.prompt everywhere (#540).
label
Above the field: .pl-label recipe (10px, tracked capitals, --oc-text-muted), tied with for. A placeholder is an example value, never the label.
validation
Say what to correct, inline, under the field, in --oc-coral text on a --oc-ground-raised line with a 2px coral left edge (the base State error recipe). Never a toast for a field error.
search
Search lives in the agents drawer, not the header (#428); a small dropdown button ([data-ds-mode], the base Button: the active mode's icon and a chevron) to the right of the box picks what the text searches: names (default), prompts, and context (the deepsearch plugin's). Its rows are the shared cascade menu's radio items, an icon and one word each, no description and no tooltip (DrawerSearch.svelte, search-modes.ts).
themes
--oc-ground-field, --oc-line-rule, --oc-text-primary, --oc-text-dim (placeholder), --oc-focus-lift (focus, via focus.css).
access
A visible label bound by for/id or wrapping. autocomplete="off" and spellcheck="false" for names and paths.

textarea

Multi-line input (a wrapper script, a note) uses the same field recipe with resize: vertical, white-space: pre for code, and line-height: 1.4 (.settings-wrapper-script-input). The notes drawer is a markdown editor, not a textarea pattern to copy (drawers).

select

Not the default. The default dropdown is the picker; a native <select> with the field recipe is for when the owner explicitly asks for one, or in the classic client/js toolbars that have not been ported. In a compiled surface a sort or a pick from a menu is SortControl or a CascadeMenu with radio items (sorting, menus).

radio group

One of a few choices, each a full-width row: the native radio, the name, dim right-aligned tags where the outcome differs, a hairline between rows, the checked row marked by a ground tint and an accent left edge. An unavailable choice stays visible and named, dimmed, with its reason in a title; never hidden. In a menu, a radio is a CascadeMenu node with checked, drawn in the accent when chosen.

switch

liveweb/src/lib/catalog/AutoUpdateSwitch.svelteon, and disabled while busy
use
A setting that takes effect the moment it flips. On a settings page a feature gets ONE large on/off switch beside one plain sentence of what it does, not how it works (the auto switch panel, AutoSwitchPanel.svelte, .oc-check with role="switch" with role="switch" and aria-checked). Compact surfaces (a component bar, the drawer) get only the icon form: grey when off, coloured with a small living detail when on (icons).
do not
Use a switch for something that needs a confirm step (use a button) or for one of several exclusive choices (use radios).
access
A native checkbox inside its label, or a button role="switch" with aria-checked. The state is also said in words (the label changes to the working verb while a request is in flight).

segmented control

Two or three views of one thing (the file drawer's view and edit modes) use .config-editor-mode-toggle: a row of buttons with aria-selected, the chosen one on the tint ground. It is a view switch, not a setting.

choice dropdown (select-only)

liveweb/src/lib/dropdown/ChoiceDropdown.sveltea launch profile with 24px marks: click the field or press Down
use
The SHORT-list sibling of the searchable picker (issue #1641): a choice from a handful of rows that are each more than one line, where the row must show a large mark and facts (a launch profile, an account). No typing filter. <ChoiceDropdown {items} {value} {onChange} label="...">{#snippet row(item, state)}...{/snippet}</ChoiceDropdown> (web/src/lib/dropdown/ChoiceDropdown.svelte, look in dropdown.css, classes .cdd*). The caller draws one row through the row snippet, so the closed field and every option show the same thing; LaunchProfileDropdown.svelte is the worked example. Items are {id, label, disabled?, hint?}; compact makes the rows shorter. multiple makes it a set (the Widget Lab's placements): values and onToggle(id) in place of value and onChange, a pick leaves the list open, and the closed field shows the caller's one-line summary (.cdd__summary). The panel placement and the option stepping are shared with the picker (placement.ts, listbox-nav.ts).
behaviour
Closed: Down, Up, Enter, Space or Alt+Down open on the chosen row; Home and End open on the first or last; a letter opens and jumps to the next row starting with it. Open: arrows, Home, End, PageUp and PageDown move through every row, a refused row included (dimmed, announced "not available", it cannot be chosen); Enter or Space choose; Tab closes without choosing. Escape closes only the list: while open the component listens on window in the capture phase, ahead of the modal stack, so Escape inside a dialog does not close the dialog; with the list closed Escape is left alone. At 767px and below the trigger is 44px tall and the panel is a full-width sheet.
look
The closed field is .oc-field in mono with the shared focus rule; the panel wears .popup-focus; options are at least 48px, hairline separated, the chosen row carries the --oc-orange bar and the active row the hover ground. Tokens only.
access
role="combobox" on the trigger with aria-expanded, aria-controls and aria-activedescendant; role="listbox" and role="option" rows with aria-selected, aria-posinset, aria-setsize and aria-disabled.

searchable picker (combobox)

liveweb/src/lib/byoai/ModelPicker.sveltethe destination kinds, grouped, a mark on each row: click the field
use
THE DEFAULT DROPDOWN: use it for EVERY choice list, short or long, unless told otherwise. A native <select> (the OS menu, no logo, no groups, off-theme) only when the owner asks for one by name (Alex (example-owner), 2026-10-03: "this should be the default unless said to do otherwise"). It started on the ai settings (BYOAI, #1201): a model, a connection, a voice, a provider preset, and now the destination kind of the notifications form. <ModelPicker items={rows} value={id} onChange={(id) => ...} label="model" /> (web/src/lib/byoai/ModelPicker.svelte, rules in picker-logic.ts, look in model-picker.css, classes .ai-pick*). Never a datalist either: it filters by the field's current text, so a field already holding a model offered only that model (the "only 1" bug). Rows are PickItems; modelItems() builds them from a connection's models and kindItems() (notifications/destinations/form-logic.ts) from the destination kinds.
logo slot
Set preset on a row to a mark id and the one-colour service logo draws before the label and in the closed field (ProviderMark, ai provider logos). Add a new service's mark to provider-marks.ts (currentColor path data, source and license in the NOTICE); no remote image, ever. A row without preset draws no mark. vendor is the group header (chat, push, incident, sms, other for the kinds; local, cloud, cli for the presets); give every row the same vendor for one flat group.
props
grouped (default true) orders recent, popular, then everything else grouped by vendor; false is a flat list in the order given. alpha={false} keeps the groups and rows in the order given. allowCustom adds a "use '<typed id>'" row when nothing matches exactly (Bedrock takes typed ids). emptyLabel adds a first "not set" row (id ''). loading, disabled.
behaviour
Opens on focus or click with the WHOLE list, whatever the field holds; the current value wears an accent bar. Typing filters: case-insensitive, every space-separated word must match the id, shown text, name or vendor, and matches are underlined in --oc-orange-bright. Only the rows in view are drawn (fixed row heights), so 500 models stay smooth. Keys: arrows, Home, End, PageUp, PageDown, Enter, Escape. At 767px and below the panel is a full-width sheet under the field.
look
The closed field is .oc-field in mono. The open panel wears .popup-focus (never its own border, ground or shadow), fixed-positioned from the field's box so no scrolling parent clips it. Section labels are the small tracked uppercase title-row strip, --oc-orange for recent and popular and dim for vendor groups; rows are hairline separated; flags are 1px outlined chips. Tokens only.
access
role="combobox" on the input with aria-activedescendant, role="listbox" and role="option" rows with aria-posinset and aria-setsize. data-count on the listbox is the number of options, drawn or not.

common mistakes

  • A rounded field with an accent outline. PR #598's field read as a red-outlined box. A field is a hairline; its focus is cyan.
  • A checkbox that repaints its row when ticked. The row stays still; the tick is the signal (#592).
  • An explanation paragraph above the controls. One sentence beside the switch; the rest folds shut in a <details> (#535, #558).
  • A custom-drawn checkbox or radio. It loses keyboard, form and screen reader behaviour. Tint the native one.
