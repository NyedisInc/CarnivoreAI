title rows, labels and panels

The Military Terminal unit of layout: a region with a title-row strip on its own ground, a hairline under it drawn as an inset shadow, one small tracked uppercase label in the accent, and content below split by hairlines. When a column holds several sections, each one gets its own panel (see a column of sections). Copy web/src/lib/ledger/ledger-chrome.css.

title row
.pl-titlebar: flex row, 10px gap, min-height: var(--oc-titlerow-h) (37px), padding 0 12px, --pl-titlerow ground, box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 0 var(--pl-line). A count, a status or a right-justified action sits on the same line as the title (the per-row action rule from #151).
title
.pl-title: --oc-fs-title (12px), weight 400, --oc-tracking-title, uppercase, --pl-accent, one line with an ellipsis. It is the ONE accent-coloured thing in the region. Use a real heading element (h2 or h3) and point the region's aria-labelledby at it.
label
.pl-label: --oc-fs-micro (10px), --oc-tracking-label, uppercase, --pl-dim. Field labels, fact labels (<dt class="pl-label">), column heads.
panel
.pl-panel: --pl-panel ground, 1px --pl-line-panel edge, --pl-radius. A panel exists because its content is one task or decision; a panel that exists for decoration should be a divider.
outside the ledger
These classes are scoped under .pledger, which declares the --pl-* aliases. A new compiled page puts class="pledger" on its root and uses them directly (building a new page). A legacy dialog that cannot reach them restates the recipe and names the source (.text-prompt-modal__head, .restart-picker__head). The operations center cells draw the same recipe through CellFrame (cells).
themes
Through the --pl-* aliases: --oc-ground-titlerow, --oc-ground-panel, --oc-line-rule, --oc-line-panel, --oc-orange, --oc-text-muted.
ascii themes
A theme that sets --ascii-display: block decorates the dashboard's frames with static text rails at a chosen density (ArtFrame.svelte, art/ascii-art.css, docs/ascii-theme-layout.md). Decoration never enters a reading zone and never captures a pointer; a new panel needs no ASCII work of its own when it is built on CellFrame.

one section, one card
Each section is its own .oc-card (Card.svelte, base components), a sibling in the column, never a card inside another card's body. This is the one reconciling line with the hairline rule: a region of one thing is split by hairlines, a column of several sections is split into panels. One bordered level, no drop shadow, no hover lift, the theme's small corner (--oc-radius-panel).
the strip
.oc-card__head: a fixed height (--oc-titlerow-h-short, 31px) on the band ground, its hairline an inset shadow. .oc-card__title is the small tracked uppercase accent label, the ONE accent thing in the section; a short note (a count, a version) sits at the right in .oc-card__meta, muted. A section with no strip is not a section.
the gap
24px (--oc-space-5) between sections, set once as the column's gap; 12px (--oc-space-3) or less inside a section. The gap between sections is always the biggest step in the column, so the eye groups by it.
the primary section
Exactly one per column: the section that holds the next step, placed first. Its strip is taller (40px, calc(var(--oc-space-6) + var(--oc-space-2))) on the --oc-orange-tint ground with its rule in --oc-strip-edge, the card's edge is --oc-strip-edge, and a 3px --oc-orange edge runs down its left side. No other section wears any of it, and nothing in its body turns accent: the edge is the one primary thing the one-accent rule allows.
the worked example
The warez publish page's detail column (#2469): the next step, the 12 checks, the timeline, versions and rules, each under its own strip, the next step primary. Until #2469 lands, the specimen above draws the primary marking inline on the base card; the publish page's own panel joins this section when it lands.
judge it

common mistakes

  • The title in plain bold at 1.3rem. That is the legacy .modal-header; every restyled surface uses the tracked accent label instead.
  • Cards inside cards. Nested boxes read as a web dashboard. Split with hairlines.
  • A second accent on the surface. If the title is accent, nothing else in the region is, except the one primary action's edge.
  • Accent-coloured dividers. Dividers between list items are grey (--oc-line-faint), like the agents listing.
  • No primary section, or two. When every strip is equally loud nothing says where to start; when two sections wear the tint and the lit edge, neither leads. One per column: the next step.
  • A gap between sections no bigger than the gaps inside them. The column then reads as one long section. 24px between, 12px or less inside.
