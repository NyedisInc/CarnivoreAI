open sessions
Each card lists the open sessions running under that account (LedgerSessions, .ledger-sessions): a .pl-label caption, then one hairline-separated row per session with its status light and name; a click opens the session. common mistakes

  • A table for items the user decides about one at a time. Cards (#535).
  • A status by colour alone. The chip says the state; its colour repeats it.
  • A reset time printed as 12:59. Round to the nearest minute (resetParts).
  • A meter split across two lines. Bar, percent and reset time stay on one line per window.
  • A management page with its own list, search or switch. Mount the manager frame and write an adapter; a second implementation is the junk drawer the frame exists to prevent (#763).
  • A setting the form shows as its default filled in and saved. Absent is not a default: an untouched field is not stored, and "use default" clears a choice rather than writing the default (#888).
  • Hiding what cannot be installed. A catalog that hides things is indistinguishable from one that lacks them; list untrusted items with the reason.
