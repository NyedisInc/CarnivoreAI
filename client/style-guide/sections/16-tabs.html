tabs and tab strips

page tabs

The strip a PAGE wears under its title bar: Tabs.svelte (web/src/opscenter/ui/), placed through Page's tabs snippet. It is not the terminal strip below, and it is not for filters: filters are .oc-chip with aria-pressed.

  • Props. tabs (id, translated lowercase label, optional icon, badge count, dot, disabled), active, onSelect, label (the tablist's name), idBase. The host spreads panelAttrs(idBase, id) on its panel. Tabs owns no URL; the host maps its hash to active and remembers the choice with tab-memory.ts.
  • Access. Roving tabindex, arrows wrap, Home and End jump, disabled tabs are skipped, and selection follows focus. Focus is the shared rule on the tab's reserved 1px border.
  • Sizes. 31px at 1024 and up, 44px below. Tabs never wrap: the strip scrolls sideways with a faded edge. The selected tab is the LIGHTER fill (--oc-ground-tab-selected) with the accent top edge, and the other tabs the darker one (--oc-ground-tab-inactive, a step lighter under the pointer); the selected tab does not change on hover (Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-12, #1689: "the current tab should be the lighter color and the darker color should be the background tabs"). That is the FULL-PAGE rule ("A"). Held by tests/python/design_system/test_page_band_layers.py. The badge is a dim count, the dot the unsaved-draft mark. Any other sideways row (the skills filter row) wears the same fade through use:scrollFade (ui/scroll-fade.ts), which sets .oc-fade-start and .oc-fade-end.

widget tabs, joined to the body

Tabs INSIDE A WIDGET (the developer widget's pipeline and scheduled tabs) are joined: the picked tab wears the widget body's ground, --oc-ground-panel, with no hairline between it and the body. The strip's empty space and the other tabs wear ONE ground, --oc-ground-tabstrip-joined, clearly DARKER than the body (a fixed 0.08 step of oklab lightness, so a light theme steps darker too; a ground already near black, such as matrix, steps lighter instead, because there is nothing darker), with a hairline under them; an unselected tab under the pointer steps back toward the body (--oc-ground-tab-joined-hover). Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-14, #1689: "B in widgets only, A on full pages", and on the real developer widget: "the first tab should match the color of the card". It is the same Tabs.svelte: a strip inside CellFrame (.oc-frame) gets it with no prop; a widget docked in the session rail under a terminal (.rail-body) gets it the same way; joined (class oc-ptabs--joined) asks for it elsewhere. A .oc-page inside a frame is a full page and keeps the lighter fill. A strip may carry ONE small control at its right end, outside the scrolling list (trailing snippet, .oc-ptabs__trailing): TM Mark II's mark read. Held by tests/python/design_system/test_page_band_layers.py and Tabs.joined.test.ts.

compact and collapse (Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-22, #1710 round 4: "tabs need to have smaller text, tab height is good ... make into icons as it gets smaller"): compact (class oc-ptabs--compact) draws the label at the micro size and keeps the strip's height; collapse (class oc-ptabs--collapse) makes the strip its own size container: a tab with an icon draws its label wide and its icon alone below 340px, with the label kept as the tab's aria-label and title (a tab with no icon keeps its label). A host that wants the picked tab to match its panel sets --_tab-on to the panel's ground. Held by Tabs.render.test.ts.

terminal tab strip

On the terminal page, a component's top row IS its tab strip: one tab per session (Alex, #452). The active tab wears the title row's ground and joins the band under it; the rest sit on a darker mix of the same ground. A tab has no height of its own: it stretches to its host's row. A right click on a tab opens that tab's own menu: the session menu, the asleep menu, the page, file or browser row menu, or a small close-tab menu for a closed page (menus, "a tab's menu").

anatomy
Left to right: the component's icon (a button that opens the component options menu), the session's status light (THE one StatusLed), the provider mark only when the session has a provider, the name (truncates), and the "x". After the last tab, set off by a gap, the dimmed "+" that opens the "+" tab menu (#453, #514).
props
items (TabItem[]: id, name, family, tint, and the light's status, unread, startupGate, statusSource, transport), active, onSelect, onClose, onKill, onNew and newOpen, onIconClick and menuOpen, group, dragging, dropIndex, onDragStart, touch, menuLabel. Omit onClose and there is no "x"; omit onNew and there is no "+".
behaviour
Clicking a tab changes the view and nothing else: the grid, the URL and the page's session stay put. The "x" closes the tab only; holding Shift, or a 500ms long press on touch, turns it into the skull, which kills the session through the one existing kill path (it confirms first). Delete closes a focused tab, Shift+Delete kills. A tab that does not fit shrinks to a minimum with its title truncated, then the strip scrolls sideways with faded edges. Dragging lifts a tab past a threshold, so a click is still a click (engine/tab-drag.ts, #455).
sizes
The strip row is as tall as the title row under it: 31px on a terminal component (titleHeight in the terminal's meta, --oc-titlerow-h-short), 44px on the phone strip. 5px above it (.oc-frame-topgap, #606); the band under it sits flush, 0px (Alex, #627, reversing #594's 5px). Every icon is 14x14; the Shift skull is the one exception at 16x16 (Alex, #606) and never changes the tab's height.
one band
In a tabbed component the active tab and the prompt band under the strip are ONE continuous surface: both compute to --oc-ground-titlerow, with no divider under the band (Alex, #606 follow-up). An untabbed card's title row keeps its divider. The gap ABOVE the strip is a DIFFERENT surface (Alex, #627: REVERSES the #606 ruling that gave it the active tab's colour): it shares --oc-ground-tabstrip with the strip's own empty area instead.
the tool icon group
The component's tool icons (skill cap, cube, folder, search, doc, palette, image, keep-awake cup, the kebab) render INSIDE THIS STRIP now, right-aligned after the tabs and the "+", vertically centred with them (Alex, 2026-09-25, #627, superseding "move all the icons down to the right of the arrows" from the #533 prompt-bar merge - see this guide's "terminal chrome and the prompt bar" section, "tools" entry). They act on the active tab, fold into the kebab at a narrow component width exactly as before, and the tabs' own shrink-then-scroll leaves them room: they never overlap the icon group or the "+".
zoom
Shift+Click the search icon (magnifying glass) and it zooms this component to fill the whole grid, temporarily, for this device only - never written to terminal_layouts or any synced state, so a reload always returns to the ordinary layout (Alex, 2026-09-26, #819). Holding Shift over it swaps the glass to the expand icon in --oc-cyan, the same mechanism the tab strip's own skull uses (lib/shift-held.svelte.ts), and only when the layout holds more than one component - on a layout of one, Shift+Click is a plain click. While zoomed a leave-full-screen icon (shrink) appears right of the magnifying glass; clicking it, or Shift+Esc (captured before xterm can see it, and only while something is zoomed - a plain Esc always reaches the terminal), restores the exact prior tabs, sizes and positions. The other components stay mounted and connected, just covered, and stop voting on their terminal's pane size while covered (terminal-group/tab-vote.ts's followTab, the same wire flag issue #454 built for a hidden tab).
themes
--oc-ground-tabstrip behind the strip, --oc-ground-titlerow on the active tab, an inactive tab's session accent (tint) mixed into the strip ground, weaker than the step to the active tab. Every colour is a token or a color-mix of one.
access
The WAI-ARIA tabs pattern: a tablist of tabs, aria-selected on the one showing, a roving tabindex so the strip is one Tab stop, arrows, Home and End move between tabs, selection follows focus. The "x" is outside the roving order. The "+" is a menu button with aria-expanded, outside the tablist.
page tabs
A tab can hold an opened page (a review board is one), an open file or a proxy browser, not only a session (Joe, #1787): the key is page:<id>, the pane is PageTabBody.svelte (.oc-pg, the frame, the file editor or the browser view, embedded), and a closed item shows a quiet base state line. The tab wears a glyph for its kind and no status light. A saved Layout stores its pages beside its sessions.
widget tabs
Any widget can open as a tab beside the terminals (slice 8b, #2008; Joe, item 1049): the key is widget:<widget id>, the pane is WidgetTabBody.svelte (.oc-wt, the widget drawn through the one widget shell), and a widget this build does not carry shows a quiet base state sentence. The tab wears the widget's icon, title and the one ZoneBadge on the icon (.oc-tab-badgehost, the globe for an all sessions face), never a status light. The "+" menu's widget root lists the widget picker by category; a right-click or the icon opens the widget's own menu (its cell's declared actions, the scope switch, close tab). A hidden widget tab makes no requests. The phone strip does not show widget tabs.
zone
The component is a zone (slice 7, #2008): ZoneShell draws every tab in its own .zone-pane, stacked on one box (.zone-pane--stacked, an inactive one .zone-pane--hidden, which is also what the terminal paint gate reads), each pane its own theme scope; the strip row's tools are the ACTIVE tab's (ZoneToolbar) and a right-click on any tab opens that tab's menu.
sync
A component's tabs are synced across devices; its active tab is not (Alex, #451 to #457): the terminal_layouts preference, written only on a user operation. A tab is keyed on the session row, not the app's session id (#612).

the other tab rows

  • Phone strip. Below 1024px web/src/opscenter/phone/PhoneTabs.svelte replaces the grid: the same TabStrip with touch, one pane at a time (PhoneTabPane.svelte). It is also the phone's only top bar (the page header is collapsed by client/css/phone-tabs-bar.css): the Carnivore claw is pinned left and opens the agents drawer, the tabs scroll in the middle, and the alert bell (with its count) and a bare kebab with no border are pinned right, every target at least 44px. The kebab presses the header menu's own toggle, so there is one menu.
  • Drawer tabs. Not a strip any more: the agents drawer's tabs are keys on the sidebar rail (SidebarRail.svelte), icon only - see the sidebar rail.
  • Settings sections. Not tabs any more: the settings page has a section rail (a column, and a chip strip on a phone) - see settings.
  • Drop overlay. DropOverlay.svelte marks where a dragged tab would land in another component.

common mistakes

  • Mixed icon sizes in a tab. Misaligned tab icons were reworked twice (#514, #569). 14x14, centred.
  • A tab click that navigates. It swaps the visible pane only; the drawer is what navigates.
  • An "x" that kills. Close and kill are two acts; kill needs Shift or a long press and a confirm.
  • A "+" menu that lists what is already open. Excluded since #596.
  • A strip outside its host. Mounted bare, a tab collapses to its text height; render it in CellFrame's strip row or the phone host.
  • Growing the row for the skull. The tab height never changes.
