The frame every screen sits in. client/index.html is ONE document: the screens are divs, one visible at a time (.screen.active), under one sticky header, with the agents drawer on the left and one shared right-hand slot for the file or notes drawer. Escape (rail layout) and the phone back arrow close the page; a page that holds a typed value waiting for a save registers a check (menu-page-dirty.ts, today Settings) and the close first asks through the app's confirm dialog. Below 1024px (no surface to measure) the page keeps the old full-screen cover: menu-page.css starts it at the top and leaves the screen behind, the legacy header and the right drawers visibility: hidden. Where the drawer is neither docked nor railed, MenuPageBar.svelte's .menu-page-bar strip holds the claw (.menu-page-bar__claw, the rail's own) as the door to the drawer, and the page starts below it; below 768px a page drawn by Page carries its own back arrow (.oc-titlebar__back), the shell strip is not drawn and the page starts at the top; any other page keeps the strip, whose claw is then a back arrow (a base quiet IconButton). A new menu page is one entry in menu-pages.ts and its container id in the stylesheet's two lists; menu-page-shell.test.ts fails when they differ.

common mistakes

  • A new screen that joins the legacy screen family. That teaches two legacy modules a fifth name. Build an overlay with its own container (building a new page).
  • A page that starts below --overlay-top on its own. The header that token measures is not on every screen; on the terminal page the strip above an overlay was the session's tab strip, live and clickable. A menu page takes the column (the menu page shell).
  • Search in the header. It lives in the drawer (#428).
  • A second bucket for a concept that already has a home. Joe's UNGROUPED band was rejected (#512).
  • Drawer boxes that narrate. A drawer box carries only what you act on; a countdown such as "login expires in 27d" lives on the full ledger page (PR #548).
  • Keying on a tmux name. A session's identity is its uuid; a rename or a recreate changes the name (gotcha 4b).

page frame

Page.svelte (web/src/opscenter/page.css): the title bar is the terminal's own 37px row (one 44px row below 1024: the title never truncates, the sub truncates and goes under 480px, actions scroll sideways). Optional slots: tabs (a Tabs strip, .oc-page__tabs), bar (min 37px), foot (a 24px strip pinned to the bottom, .oc-page__foot). INSET: a host calls provideInset() (web/src/opscenter/ui/page-inset.ts) and every Page below it draws no title bar; its sub and actions ride at the right end of the bar row (.oc-page__inset-end).

page layers: the band and the data

the rule
Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-03: "the search, filters, etc theme coloring should always house the top layer and the other lower color should have the data". Every page has TWO grounds, both from the theme. The BAND (--oc-ground-band, the theme's chrome ground) holds the title bar (.oc-titlebar), the tab strip, the search and filter bar (.oc-page__bar, also the inset bar of an embedded screen) and the foot (.oc-page__foot). The CONTENT ground (--oc-ground-content, the theme's page ground) is the page root and everything in the body. The tab strip itself stays its own darker step of the band (--oc-ground-tabstrip), and on it the selected tab is the lighter fill (--oc-ground-tab-selected, Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-12; see "page tabs"). The bar's first line may be one sentence saying what the page is (.oc-page__lede, secondary text, never the accent; the dashboards page uses it, Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-03).
how
Use Page.svelte and its slots and the layers come free. A page-specific row that belongs to the top band paints --oc-ground-band; a region of data never does. Never --oc-ground-panel for a band: it is the theme's --color-bg, which on some themes is lighter than the chrome and on others equals the page, so the band either goes off-theme or disappears.
