contrast: every theme, every role

A role matrix, measured live in this browser: every text role on every ground, and the non-text pairs a base must guarantee, under every shipped theme (issue #2033). Each pair is resolved on a real element painted with that theme alone, composited over the page ground, and scored with the WCAG 2.x formula, so color-mix and relative colors count exactly as they are painted. This page reports; it does not fail a build. The gate that holds bundled themes to these floors comes in a later slice.

floors
Text 4.5:1 on every ground (WCAG AA). A text pair between 3:1 and 4.5:1 is flagged "large only": readable as a heading, not as body text. The control line (--oc-line-control) needs 3:1 against its ground (WCAG 1.4.11). A hover or selected layer needs a visible step of 1.1:1 off its surface. The hairline (--oc-line-hairline) is decorative and has no floor; its ratio is shown for reference.
resting and transient grounds
Sunken, base, band, raised, and the legacy panel and card grounds are resting: something sits on them all the time. The overlay and the legacy hover ground (marked *) are transient. The first count in the table is resting failures, the ones a reader meets on an ordinary page.
