scrollbars

Scrollbars are themed once, globally, by client/css/scrollbars.css, for pointer devices ((hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)). A component never styles its own. Always reachable: a thin themed thumb is drawn whenever the area actually overflows, and brightens under the pointer with no layout shift.

livethe shipped scrollbar on an ordinary scrolling box, point at the thumb

at rest the thumb is already drawn (--oc-scrollbar-thumb-rest) and the track is quiet - nothing appears or moves when the pointer arrives.

the full thumb colour is the theme's accent pulled 70 percent of the way onto its own ground (--oc-scrollbar-thumb).

hovering the thumb itself takes it to 85 percent (--oc-scrollbar-thumb-hover) and holding it takes it all the way to the accent (--oc-scrollbar-thumb-active).

the track, once shown, is the theme's own text colour at 8 percent (--oc-scrollbar-track), so the channel reads as cut into the surface.

the bar is 11px wide with a 2px transparent inset, so the whole 11px is grabbable and 7px of it is painted, with a 36px minimum thumb.

measured thumb against track across all 40 themes: worst 2.19 (snes), carnivore 2.83, best 7.77 (matrix).

a box that does not overflow has no bar at all.

use
Any box with overflow: auto or scroll gets the themed, always-reachable bar automatically. Give a page ONE scrolling box (the page frame's .oc-page__body): a fixed overlay whose document never scrolls under it.
do not
Declare ::-webkit-scrollbar rules in a component, hide a scroll region's ability to scroll (this file only ever changes paint, never overflow or display), or set the background shorthand on the thumb (it resets background-clip and fills the inset back in).
why never hover on the box
themes
Reads --oc-scrollbar-thumb-rest, --oc-scrollbar-thumb, --oc-scrollbar-thumb-hover, --oc-scrollbar-thumb-active and --oc-scrollbar-track, all derived from the theme's accent, ground and text. A fixed red would be wrong on matrix, gameboy, the ASCII themes and codex.
terminal
xterm's own scrollbar is drawn at zero width by client/css/styles.css; the 16px the fit addon reserves for it is where --oc-terminal-inset comes from.
