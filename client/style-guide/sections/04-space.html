spacing, geometry, radii, borders, elevation

Carnivore's geometry is fixed pixels measured off the reference mockup, held in web/src/opscenter/tokens.css. Surfaces are split by 1px hairlines, not by cards and shadows. Corners are small and square.

spacing
Steps of 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14 and 16px cover everything. 12px is the phone gutter and 16px the desktop gutter (--pl-gutter). Prefer more space over less, with grey dividers between list items. A title row pads 0 12 to 14px; a panel body pads 12px; a dialog footer 12 to 16px.
between sections
hairlines
1px (--oc-border) in --oc-line-rule between regions, --oc-line-faint between list rows, --oc-line-panel for a panel or quiet control edge. A title row's rule is an inset shadow (box-shadow: inset 0 -1px 0 0 var(--oc-line-rule)), which costs no layout; never a border that moves the row by a pixel.
radii
2px on controls (buttons, fields, chips), --oc-radius-sm (4px) on quiet boxes and dialogs, --oc-radius (6px) on a panel. Three themes zero every radius, so nothing may depend on a rounded corner. Never a pill-shaped button.
elevation
Ground steps and hairlines separate surfaces. The only shadows are the popup-focus drop shadows under a floating menu, --shadow-modal under the legacy status pill and attach menu, and --oc-shadow-lift under a panel being dragged. The dialog chassis has none: its ground and hairline separate it. No card hover lift, no glow on buttons. Surfaces step off the content ground by the surface ladder (--oc-surface-1, -1-on, -2, --oc-line-surface), a fixed share toward the theme's ink, so a tile, panel or card never paints a raw manifest ground.
breakpoints
1023px is the design contract's phone line: 44px targets, stacked panels, 12px gutter. 769px decides whether the phone-only floating controls exist. 700px decides whether the agents sidebar and the right drawer dock or overlay. 900px is the archive's own column line. Smaller adjustments live at 768, 640, 600 and 480. Cap and centre content on very wide screens (max-width: 1680px; margin: 0 auto), never stretch it edge to edge at 2560.
tap targets
Every control a thumb can reach is at least 44px (--oc-tap) at the phone line, set as a min-height floor so a wrapping label still grows the row. A pointer-only desktop control may be 30px.

common mistakes

  • Tidying a measured number. The dialog chassis pads 31px, not 30, because a 2px frame became a 1px hairline and the pixel moved into the padding to hold both the outer box and the content box still (client/css/styles.css, above .modal-overlay). Re-measure before rounding.
  • A border for a rule. A 1px border on a title row pushes the body down a pixel and shifts every measured position under it. Draw the rule as an inset shadow.
  • Horizontal scroll at 390px. A fixed min-width, an unbroken token or a table without a scroll wrapper. Give long strings overflow-wrap: anywhere and wrap tables.
  • A layout jump on a state change. The restart picker's footer wrapped when "restart" became "kill and restart" and the centred modal shifted every row above it (#592). Size the container for the longest label of every state.
