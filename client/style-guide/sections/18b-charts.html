charts

Every analytics chart is drawn by one component, web/src/opscenter/cells/analytics/ChartCanvas.svelte (one selectively imported ECharts instance). A chart colours by SERIES, never by query, so a grouped metric is readable.

series palette

use
Each drawn series takes the next of eight categorical colours, --oc-series-1 to --oc-series-8, in draw order. A ninth series and beyond cycle the same eight and are drawn dashed, so line style is a second channel. A comparison ("before") series keeps its parent's colour and is always dashed. Status hues (green, coral) are never used to tell series apart: they mean state.
colour
Eight fixed hues spaced round the wheel; the lightness comes from the theme's own ground, so marks are lighter on a dark theme and darker on a light one. tests/python/design_system/test_chart_series_palette.py proves, for every shipped theme, that all eight are pairwise distinct and clear the ground by 3:1. The chart resolves them to sRGB before handing them to ECharts (series-colors.ts), because its renderer cannot parse oklch().

legend toggle

use
The legend is interactive: click a name to hide its series, click again to show it. The choice survives a redraw (new data, a theme switch, a resize) because the chart merges the legend's selected state into every draw instead of resetting it. A series that no longer exists drops out of the remembered state.
compact
A compact chart (the tile and panel tiers of a home widget) shows no legend and no toggle; the full tier and the details dialog show both.
type
Chart text uses --font-mono, like the rest of the terminal look.

renderers and density

use
An analytics widget is drawn by ONE renderer chosen by its saved viz (timeline, kpi, leaderboard, treemap or heat; a row saved before the choice existed is a timeline). web/src/opscenter/cells/analytics/Cell.svelte only dispatches, through web/src/opscenter/viz/registry.ts. A new renderer is one component, one entry in viz/specs.ts and one line in the registry; it never adds its own fetching.
density
There is no density setting. The measured box picks a tier with lib/states.ts::pickState (12px hysteresis): tile is the figure and a sparkline, panel adds key readouts, full adds the legend, markers and details. Size a widget to change what it shows.
kpi
KpiRenderer.svelte: the figure in --oc-fs-display, the change against the period before as a WORD and a percent (never an arrow alone), coloured by token (--oc-yellow up, --oc-cyan down, muted for flat), a SparkLine.svelte trend whose missing values are gaps and never zeros, and hairline-separated readouts. One accent only: the delta colour. Units come from the catalog: SI suffixes, USD, durations.
leaderboard
LeaderboardRenderer.svelte: a ranked table of any dimension (group_by on its series, top_n option, 1 to 10). Each row is rank, name, a bar in the shared .oc-gauge fill form sized by the row's share of the largest, the value and, at the full tier, its share and a SparkLine.svelte trend. Tile shows rank, name and value only; panel adds the bar. Hairlines between rows, no cards.
agents
AgentsRenderer.svelte: tokens per agent. The timeline line plot, one line per agent (the top eight of the window, named by their session, each in the Swarm colour helper's stable hue) and the rest summed into one dashed "other agents" line in --oc-text-dim, with the tokens axis and the window named. The 1h, 24h and 1w choice is the shared .oc-seg in the host header. Below the plot a hairline and a small muted label open the live list: running agents only, a swatch in the agent's hue, name, a thin .oc-gauge bar and tokens per minute over the last five minutes, highest first; a plain line says so when nothing is using tokens.
treemap
TreemapRenderer.svelte (ECharts treemap): area is tokens or estimated cost (measure option), nested provider, model, project. Leaves take their provider's --oc-series-* colour, in three shades by model. Tile shows providers, panel adds models under a caption per provider, full adds projects. A model's area always equals its ranked value; the part its top projects do not cover is one leaf named "other".
heat
HeatRenderer.svelte (ECharts heatmap): hour of day by weekday of any metric, each cell the mean of the hours that landed there; an hour with no reading is empty, never zero. Six steps of --oc-series-5 mixed into --oc-ground-panel, so the ramp follows every theme; no VisualMap component. Covers at most the last 24 days.
vitals
VitalsRenderer.svelte: a NOC strip of live figures (classes viz-vitals, viz-vitals__grid, viz-vitals__cell): live sessions, tokens per minute with a rising, steady or falling word (5 minute burn against 60), and, from the panel tier, estimated dollars per hour, 15 minute cache hit and subagents running; full adds the working, waiting and idle counts with the shared StatusLed. Big numerals in --oc-fs-display. An unmeasured figure reads "unknown", never zero. Reads /stats/live and the sessions listing; no series.
hot
HotRenderer.svelte (classes viz-hot, viz-hot__row, viz-hot__bar): "top for agents", the busiest conversations of the last 15 minutes as project, model, share bar (the shared .oc-gauge fill form), provider, tokens and cost. A row whose conversation id is exactly one live session's claude_session_uuid opens that session through openSession (viz-hot__open, the info colour); a project name never links, and a row with no match stays plain text. Two rows of one project carry a dim viz-hot__tag after the name (the live session's title, else the conversation id's first six characters) so they can be told apart; the model column prints the readable model name ("Opus 5.5"), never the id.
events
EventsRenderer.svelte (classes viz-events, viz-events__row, viz-events__kind): a ticker of the typed event log and the annotations, newest first and bounded to 50, icon-free text rows of time, kind label (the accent, the one accent on the surface) and subject; scrolls inside its box. Follows stats.changed and stats.tripwire. All three live kinds refresh on a 15 second floor, only while on screen and the tab is visible.
fleet
FleetRenderer.svelte (classes viz-fleet, viz-fleet__rack, viz-fleet__row, viz-fleet__summary): a rack of every live session, those waiting on you first. Each row is the shared StatusLed given all its signals (unread, startup gate, status source, this browser's transport for the session), the name (the info colour, opens the session through openSession), project, model (full), the context fill in the shared LimitGauge (nothing drawn when unmeasured) and the subagents running. tile is the count by state only.
pack
PackRenderer.svelte (classes viz-pack, viz-pack__box, viz-pack__bar, viz-pack__line, viz-pack__total): where the agents run and what each computer carries. One block per computer in the Pack, the master marked with the one accent label, the active reserve level in the info colour, and a load bar (busy cores over cores) with the reserve line drawn on it at the usable capacity; the fill turns to the danger colour past the line. Under it cpu, memory, disk, battery and the agent count with the cores and memory they use, then the heaviest agents. A closing line totals the live computers, and full adds a table of every agent with its computer. A computer with no fresh snapshot says its state in words and draws no figure (never zero). It reads the Pack roster store, pushed on pack_machines, with no timer while the push is up, and shares no clock with any other widget. Colours are --oc-* tokens only.
runway
swimlanes
SwimlanesRenderer.svelte (ECharts custom series; classes viz-swim, viz-swim__canvas, viz-swim__key): one lane per active conversation over the range, blocks of working (green), waiting (yellow) and idle (muted) from the activity minutes, with a thin cyan strip along each block's foot whose opacity is the subagent density. A bucket nobody measured is a gap. Lanes are named after the live session carrying that conversation id. No animation.
flow
FlowRenderer.svelte (ECharts Sankey; classes viz-flow, viz-flow__canvas, viz-flow__key): where the range's tokens or estimated cost went, account to model to agent kind to project. At most twelve queries per refresh (flow.ts): the top three of three levels, then each drilled once with a filter. A link is exact for its own pair of levels; what falls outside a level's top three is one "other" node. tile draws account and model, panel adds agent kind, full adds project and labels.
scatter
ScatterRenderer.svelte (ECharts scatter; classes viz-scatter, viz-scatter__canvas, viz-scatter__key): the outlier hunter, one dot per conversation (the 500 costliest), duration on x, estimated cost on y, both logarithmic, tokens as the dot's area, project and model on hover. A dot opens its session only when exactly one live session carries that conversation id (cyan is plain, green is openable). A conversation with no known price is counted in the caption, never drawn at zero.
impact
ImpactRenderer.svelte (classes viz-impact, viz-impact__verdict, viz-impact__grid, viz-impact__flag): one marker, a per-conversation metric, N days each side. The two medians, the change, a seeded 95 percent bootstrap range and a yellow flag when the model mix moved a lot. It says "correlation, not proof", never a cause; too few conversations reads "not enough", never a verdict.
health
HealthRenderer.svelte (classes viz-health, viz-health__grid, viz-health__state): the stats collector's own vital signs from the coverage read: state with the age of its last pass (coral past 45 seconds), backlog and replay, newest usage, missing sources, database size. Unmeasured reads unknown, never zero or fine.
empty
settings
VizSettings.svelte: the edit-layout strip of the leaderboard, treemap, heat and live-ops renderers (title, renderer, metric, grouping, range, enabled, plus the renderer's own option), through changeAnalyticsConfig so undo and cancel cover it.
gallery
Widgets are added from the edit-layout drawer's gallery (WidgetDrawer.svelte, classes le-gallery, le-gallery__cats, le-gallery__cards, AnalyticsFamilyFold.svelte): five categories (live ops, flow and spend, agents, change, system), one card per preset, presets being data in viz/presets.ts. A new widget lands next to the cell last selected, else at the end; a full card takes a row of its own.
alarm
A tripwire on a widget shows on every renderer through cells/analytics/Cell.svelte, in the pop-out too. firing turns the widget's own 1px border coral (.alarm-frame--firing, --oc-coral, no glow, no animation) and adds an AlarmStrip.svelte row (.alarm-strip--firing) reading the WORD "tripped" and the value against its threshold; unknown is a quiet "no data" row; ok draws nothing. A timeline draws each threshold as a dashed coral markLine, resolved to sRGB like the series colours. State is the tripwire list at each refresh tick plus live stats.tripwire frames (viz/tripwires.svelte.ts); alerts are in-app only, so nothing is ever sent outside the app.
threshold editor
TripwireEditor.svelte with TripwireRow.svelte in the edit-layout strip of the timeline, kpi and leaderboard: a hairline-topped block with the accent-coloured label, one row per tripwire (comparator, threshold in the DISPLAY unit such as USD or percent and stored in the metric's own unit through viz/tripwire-model.ts, window, hold and cooldown minutes, enabled), shared .oc-field inputs, .modal-btn-confirm to save and .modal-btn-danger to remove. Saves go straight to the tripwire routes, not the layout draft; a chart not yet saved to the server shows a note instead.
data
Every widget on screen hands its query items to viz/query-client.ts, which sends one query-batch request per refresh tick (split at 32), routes results back per widget, shows an item's error on that widget only, and holds while the tab is hidden.
