progress bars and meters

Carnivore draws progress three ways, each shown here as the real component on fixed data: the segmented quota meter, the limit gauge with its pace marker and the bottom bar meter. A new meter copies one of them (Alex (example-owner), 2026-09-25, #637: "ours are like this").

segmented quota meter

live, inertweb/src/lib/ledger-drawer/DrawerLedger.sveltefolded, then unfolded
use
An account's rate-limit window: a row of small outlined cells (.seg-bar, .seg-bar__cell), lit from the left, the percent at the right end. Folded, one line per account with 5h and 7d side by side; unfolded, one line per window with its reset. The bar itself is one shared component, web/src/lib/ui/SegmentedMeterBar.svelte: the agents drawer's PROVIDERS panel and the provider ledger screen's per-window table (LedgerUsageCell.svelte, issue #657) both render it, so there is exactly one segmented bar in the app, never a ledger-only copy of it.
colour
One five-step ladder for the segmented meter and the gauge (data-scale 1 to 5): --oc-green-bright, --oc-cyan, --oc-yellow-bright, a yellow and coral mix, --oc-coral. A window steps at 50, 60, 75 and 90% (WINDOW_SCALE_STOPS in client/js/labels/usage-panel.js). Unlit cells keep only their outline.
active row
The account the pane is spending against gets the ground tint and an accent name, never a ring.

limit gauge and pace marker

use
Every account limit window (5h, 7d, the swarm total) is drawn by ONE primitive, web/src/opscenter/ui/LimitGauge.svelte (.oc-gauge in web/src/opscenter/controls.css): a usage bar with ONE vertical PACE MARKER (.oc-gauge__pace, 2px wide) at the elapsed fraction of the window, 1 - time_to_reset / window_length (5h is five hours, 7d is seven days). It reaches 3px above and below a bar of 7px or more, 2px on a compact one, so it reads on segmented and plain bars at every size. A fill short of the marker is under pace. It has two bar forms, segments (the drawer and the ledger screen) and fill (.oc-gauge__bar, .oc-gauge__fill: the bottom bar, the grown stats, the home cell, the mini rail and the settings strips). No reset instant, or a reset already past, or a window length nobody stated, means NO marker; nothing is guessed.
colour
The USAGE BAR is coloured by usage amount alone, the absolute window ladder (data-scale: green, cyan from 50%, yellow from 60%, orange from 75%, coral from 90%); pace never touches it. The PACE MARKER (.oc-gauge__pace) is always white, the --oc-text-bright token, in every state (Alex (example-owner), 2026-10-02); in the bottom status line it stays inside the 7px bar instead of reaching above and below it. The pace state (data-pace: on, ahead, over) is still computed, once, in the pure module client/js/labels/pace.js; the spoken sentence ("5h window, 32 percent used, 60 percent of the window elapsed, on pace") is windowMeterText in usage-panel.js, also the hover title.

bottom bar meter

live, inertweb/src/opscenter/cells/terminal-live/TerminalFoot.sveltefull width, 420px, absent, unchecked
use
The terminal cell's bottom bar: context, model, tokens, then the 5h and 7d windows. Each meter is a label, a thin rounded bar (.oc-tf-bar, .oc-tf-bar-fill), the percent, then its detail or reset.
colour
The same ladder. Context steps at 25, 50, 80 and 95% (SCALE_STOPS), a window on the drawer's stops. Every step comes from sessionUsageLabels; no component keeps its own thresholds.
narrow
What the collapsed bar holds is planned by priority from measured widths (foot-plan.ts, reading a hidden measuring copy, .oc-tf-measure). Highest first: the three percents and the short work segments (never dropped), then the model name, the effort, the context fraction, the three bars (all or none), the long labels' full form, the 5h reset, the 7d reset and the work segments' full form. Each piece is taken when it still fits, so a piece too wide for what is left never stops a smaller, lower one using that room. Once the bars are dropped this way, the percent (.oc-tf-pct) keeps the scale colour its bar would have shown; the label (.oc-tf-label) is the bright text token at every width (Alex (example-owner), 2026-10-02), so only the percent says how much is used.
account tag
At the right edge (.oc-tf-acct, foot-account.ts) the short name of the provider account the session's pane runs under, from the listing wrapper's account_id; nothing when it is not measured. A yellow hazard triangle (.oc-tf-acct-warn, --oc-yellow-bright) with a tooltip sits beside it when that account is not the provider's active one. It replaces the agents count.
layout
Option A, meters first (Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-09-28, docs/terminal-foot-bar-proposal.md), one layout at every width: the three meters are one left-aligned group (.oc-tf-meters) with a hairline in the space between each present meter, and the model (.oc-tf-model) sits on the right against the work segments, so the free width collects in one place. All three bars share ONE size (Alex, 2026-09-28: "Make all of them even progress bar size"), feathered with the row from 48px to 90px (footSpacing).
context percent
The context percent (.oc-tf-pct) wears the same scale step as its bar, so the number alone says how full the conversation is once a narrow cell has dropped the bar.
reset countdown
A window's reset (.oc-tf-reset) is a recycle icon (<Icon name="recycle" />) beside the bare duration, never the word "reset"/"reset in" (Alex, 2026-09-28). The duration is always relative - "53m" under an hour, "3h 53m" under a day, "4d 3h" (days and hours only, no minutes) a day or more out - never a clock date, even far out. The icon's title/aria-label carries the full "resets in 4d 3h" sentence for a hover or a screen reader.
work segments
Right of the usage bar, in the same row (.oc-tf-line), FootWork.svelte draws up to two segments (.oc-tfw, .oc-tfw-seg) on the bar's own ground and rule: the running agents and open background shells and monitors, in working green ("agents 2 sh 1", short "ag 2 sh 1"; one name per kind, Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-09-28), and a clock with the live timer count and the time to the next fire ("3 next 32m", short "3·32m"). Each is drawn only when it has something to count, measures both of its forms (.oc-tfw-measure), and opens the session control center on its own section (cells section). They are siblings of the usage button, never inside it, because the usage bar is itself a button.
no data
Absent (nothing spent yet) draws hollow bars; unchecked (could not tell) draws hatched bars. Never a zero.

common mistakes

  • A third meter style. Copy the segmented meter, the limit gauge or the bottom bar meter.
  • A bar with no number. The percent is always printed beside it.
  • Absent drawn as 0%. Absent and unchecked each have their own look and sentence.
  • A meter split over two lines. Label, bar, percent and reset share one line.
