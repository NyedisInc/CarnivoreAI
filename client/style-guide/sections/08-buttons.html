aria-pressed="true" (a toggle) or aria-checked="true" (a radio or checkbox drawn as a button, such as a question's pills) gives any data-btn the selected ground and a bright label, in every theme.

Name the act, never "ok": session.restart.picker.cancel and session.restart.picker.go are the reference; a new dialog adds <feature>.confirm and <feature>.cancel.

A real <button type="button">; focus is a 2px cyan outline at offset 2px (.restart-picker .modal-btn:focus-visible). A disabled button says why in a title.

icon buttons

A square hit box, the icon centred and inheriting the button's colour, an aria-label and a title, the cyan focus outline. References: the tab strip's icon and close buttons (tabs) and the cell frame's controls (cells). .btn-icon is the legacy header's control class only; .modal-close is the legacy dialog "x", and new dialogs close with cancel and Escape instead.

A state never moves a pixel of layout: size, position and the row around it stay put.

long labels

A label never widens its button. A base button never grows past its container (max-width: 100%, min-width: 0). When its label does not fit, titleWhenTruncated moves the button's children into one <span data-btn-label> that clips to one line and ends in an ellipsis (a flex container cannot ellipsize its own text, so the label becomes its own block); a label that fits keeps the markup it was written with. A row trigger (data-btn="row") wraps instead, and an icon alone is not a label. Button.svelte applies the action itself; a button written by hand with buttonAttrs gives itself use:titleWhenTruncated.

Hover shows the full line only when it was cut. titleWhenTruncated (web/src/lib/base/truncate.ts) sets title to the full label while the label is clipped and takes it away again when it fits, so a button whose label fits shows nothing on hover; a title the author wrote is put back. It re-measures on resize and when the text changes. Tabs (.oc-ptab__label) and the Mark II chips use the same action.

The outline is the button line. The edge is --oc-line-button, never the hairline --oc-line-panel, which was 1.0 to 2.0:1 on 11 of 12 shipped themes and left the button with no visible outline.

common mistakes

  • A pill or a rounded accent block. PR #598's "create" was rejected.
  • Corner targeting brackets. The public site's; app buttons have none.
  • Two accent buttons side by side. One confirm per surface; the rest are quiet.
  • A label that grows and wraps the footer. Size the footer for the longest label (#592 widened the picker to 500px).
  • A local button class. Reuse .modal-btn and its variants.
