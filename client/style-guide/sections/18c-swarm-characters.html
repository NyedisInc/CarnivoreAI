swarm characters

The Swarm's agents are nodes with a face. State is shown by the BODY: the node stays still at rest, morphs into a shape that names the state (a "?" for a question, a grey gravestone for dead, a spiky square, or a plain "!" or "!!" it loops into, for an alert), and nothing is pinned on it: no badge, count or ring. The one addition is the soft even glow behind a finished_unread square. One component draws all of them, Character; the Swarm panel and the Swarm page mount it. Record and the full state table: docs/swarm.md, section 13.

fifty eye expressions

Every 120 to 180 seconds the shared clock picks ONE visible character that is at rest (idle or finished_unread, settled for 3 s) and plays one random face on it for 1.2 to 2.5 s: only the two eyes change (shape, size, tilt, offset, one quick motion), then they return to normal. Never twice in a row, never on dead, asleep, working, a question, the startup gate or an alert, never in reduced motion, and a hidden tab pauses it. Pick any name to play it now; "cycle all" runs the fifty at 1.5 s each; the sheet below holds every face still at 28 and 64 px.

the alert ladder is a body language

immediatealert_immediatethe mockup's spiky square with eyes, then a plain "!" with no spikes and no eyesmorphs to the "!" and back every 2.4 s, shaking nonstop, until answered
flashalert_flashthe spiky square with eyes, then a plain "!!", 1.3x, above its neighborsmorphs to the "!!" and back every 2 s, breaks out of its tile, jitters nonstop and flares its spikes until opened; the owner also gets a text

The spikes are one static path per rung, drawn behind the body in the body's own color and standing on its outline, so the glyph itself looks spiky. A PRIORITY badge, when there is one, lives on the Swarm drawer tab and never on a node.

use
<Character state={row.state} size={40} seed={agent.character_seed} label={agent.name} />. Sizes: agents 28, projects 40, companies 64, SysOp 96. state is one of twenty values (the eight status states, asleep, startup_gate, the Swarm events and the four alert rungs); any other value draws the hollow unknown outline, never idle. Optional: colorIndex (1 to 8, the owner's pick over the seed), subs (one ribbon per subagent seed, at most three), side (the two talking agents), swarm, still, label, tell.
no badge, one glow
tell is still accepted (the panel passes it) and draws nothing; it only adds to the spoken label. The one backdrop is the finished_unread glow, .pk-gw: a static rounded rectangle the size of the resting square with its corner radius and an equal spread on every side, in a CONTRAST series token (--gc, never the body's own color); only its opacity animates. The pixel pack draws one hard square band. On a home shape other than the square (the Swarm look, #1426) the glow drops its box and the body (.pk-bd, the SVG holding the outline) wears it as a drop shadow, so it hugs the shape.
ribbons and glyphs
A working square's ribbon turns in 0.6 of the mockup's time, is wider, and wears a bright series color that is never the body's (contrastIndex). The dead gravestone is grey (--oc-text-dim) with an engraved cross and RIP (.pk-rip) on a ground line (.pk-gl).
one attribute
One data-state per square picks every pose, keyframe and shape in web/src/cplugs/huddle/swarm/characters/characters.css, the status-led.css precedent. Exactly one animation per state, held by animation-map.test.ts.
motion
Only transform and opacity animate (plus the individual translate, scale and rotate properties), and the body shape morphs ONCE per state change through SMIL, begun one frame after the svg is inserted (a morph begun in the insertion frame never plays in Chromium). One shared clock (clock.ts) writes the gaze, the blinks and the rare idle quirk; a character at rest is still. A hidden tab pauses with animation-play-state (never animation: none), stops the clock and pauses SMIL.
reduced motion
prefers-reduced-motion sets data-still: no animation, no SMIL, the body drawn directly in its still shape (the "?" for a question, the gravestone for dead, the plain "!" glyphs for the alert rungs) and a still pose.
themes
Body colors come from the agent's color family (below); the eyes are --oc-on-accent. Carnivore's nodes render under every theme through tokens. A theme may name an alternate swarm in theme.json ("characters": {"swarm": "pixel"}); gameboy wears the pixel pack, read by readCharacterPack in web/src/cplugs/huddle/swarm/characters/pack-reader.ts, which falls back to the default swarm for a missing, malformed or unknown block.
color families
Every project, every company and SysOp wears its own hue, and only a project's agents share its tone (Alex, 2026-10-07: "only project and their agents should be similar color tones all projects companies and sysop should be different colors"). placeFamilies anchors SysOp, then every company and every project with a project agent (archived ones included) takes the free point of a 30 degree grid farthest from every taken hue, so up to 12 families sit 30 degrees apart, hue neighbours alternate lightness, a new project never moves an older one, and a project with no agent wears the next free hue without reserving it; a company's chosen hue overrides its own. A project agent, its feature and task agents and its sessions take one of 20 shades within +-10 degrees of the project's hue, with lightness and chroma steps, every pair at least 0.04 apart in OKLab, chosen by a hash of its id; the project agent wears the base hue itself. Every color is bright and vibrant (Alex: "stay away from putrid colors"): it goes through vibrantCss, the one floor (chroma 0.16 or more, lightness 0.58 or more on any ground); never emit a bare oklch for a square. A hand-set color wins as the series token it names (--oc-series-1 to --oc-series-8). Each color is oklch(from var(--oc-ground-panel) ...) on a bright base read off the ground, so it reads on every theme. ONE helper: createSwarmLive().colors or createAgentColors() (web/src/cplugs/huddle/swarm/agent-colors.svelte.ts), passed to a character as color={colors.of(agent)}; the rule is characters/family.ts (docs/swarm.md section 13.8).
access
Every character is role="img" with an accessible label from the catalog (cplug.huddle.swarm.state.*), the agent's name first. Color never carries the state alone: the body shape and motion do.

Density keys on the drawer bar map compact, normal and full to condensed, expanded and detailed; the swarm / lists switch, the density keys and a quiet grid icon key that opens the Swarm page sit in the SWARM title row (SwarmTitleActions.svelte, .pta-page; the switch is per device, the pop-out draws none).
state
One state per square, first that applies: an open alert's rung, a fresh activity (8 seconds), question_waiting, asleep, the startup gate, the listing's activity status, else unknown. The rules are pure, in swarm/panel-state.ts.
floor
A session that is a Swarm agent's body draws once, as that agent. A company or project with no agent is a VACANT SEAT (.pkp-seat on a .pkp-it--vacant button: the ghost square's dashed hairline, its name under it, no character): one click turns its agent on, no question first (Alex (example-owner), 2026-10-08, #1434: the server runs git init in a folder that is not a repo); a refusal shows its reason on one line under the section (.pkp-ask, .pkp-ask-text in the warning token). A dashed "+" slot closes the COMPANIES and PROJECTS sections (data-swarm-slot): it turns the seat on at once when one seat is vacant, else opens a choice of the vacant seats (.pkp-pick-list), and a pick turns that one on. A name takes the cell's full width on one line and cuts with an ellipsis only when longer; a detailed row always draws its provider mark, a context bar (empty when unmeasured) and its working-on line (the job when nothing is being worked on, never the state word). The default view shows ACTIVE nodes only (a live pane that is not asleep; a Swarm agent with a live body or any open tell or alert rung; a project or company holding any); the rest sit behind one quiet link at the foot (.pkp-toggle: lowercase mono, the muted text colour, never the accent), "show inactive (N)" and "hide inactive", per device. Without the plugin's swarm permission one line (.pkp-need) says so with a button to the plugins page, and the floor still draws.
drag within a tier
A node drags to a new place inside its own tier (COMPANIES, PROJECTS, AGENTS; tier-drag.ts, #1453): the mouse lifts it after a 5 px move, a finger after a 500 ms hold. The tier grid takes .pkp-drag-on (no text selection, the neighbours slide to their new slots by transform); the node takes .pkp-drag-lift, the Street View pegman: it rises over --oc-shadow-lift, grows a little and swings from the grab point. A release inside the tier glides it into the gap (.pkp-drag-home) and it settles with a small bounce (.pkp-drag-plop); Esc or a release outside the tier slides it home. Reduced motion: no swing, no slide, no bounce. The order is one server value for every device (GET / PUT /swarm/node-order, pushed as swarm_node_order), keyed on the node, never a tmux name. Shift+click stays the lineage filter and never lifts.
never
No count, dot or ring on a node; the corner tell is the only badge. The PRIORITY count (agents whose alert is priority or higher) is the drawer tab's badge. A density change never remounts a node, since a remount replays its entry.

a node's own look: the node menu

Alex (example-owner), 2026-10-08, #1452: a right-click on a Swarm agent's square (a long press on a phone) opens the node menu (NodeMenu.svelte on the shared cascade menu, so the popup-focus look and the menu-keys hotkeys come with it): r rename (the app's one text prompt, the naming rules checked by the server), c color (auto plus the eight series colors as .pk-swatch squares), b background (none plus eight tiles) and s shape (the tier default plus the Swarm look's ten shapes). The current pick is checked. Every pick is one PATCH /swarm/agents/{id}; auto, none and tier default send null.

where
The drawer's Swarm grid, the Swarm page and the org chart open it; an ordinary session's stand-in and a vacant seat have none.
reads
Every surface draws a node's shape and tile through the one resolver, shapeFor and backgroundFor in node-look.ts, passed to Character as shape and background: the drawer square, the Swarm page, the org chart, the ledger, the board faces and the chat avatar all agree. A node's own shape beats its tier's; its tile is a series color (the slot 1 to 8) mixed toward transparent drawn behind the body (data-tile, --tile).
never
Never pass a shape from the rank alone (rank= without shape={shapeFor(it)}): the node's own pick would show on one surface and not another.

common mistakes

  • Pinning anything on a node. The owner rejected notification icons everywhere: the nodes themselves become spiky. No badge, count or ring; only the finished_unread glow sits behind one.
  • A round glow behind a node. A circle is thick at the sides and thin at the corners. The halo is a rounded rectangle with the body's corner radius and equal spread.
  • A ribbon in the body's own color. Use contrastIndex, never the body's series.
  • A timer per square. The one shared clock writes the gaze and blinks; keyframes start because data-state changed.
  • Starting the morph in the insertion frame. Begin it in a following animation frame.
  • Picking a square's color anywhere but the color book. A view that calls seriesVar or a random slot breaks the company grouping; pass color={colors.of(agent)}.
  • A literal color. Body, eyes and ribbons are tokens, so every theme works.
