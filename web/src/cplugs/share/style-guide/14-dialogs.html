share plugin: the read-only link dialog

share read-only link
web/src/cplugs/share/ShareModal.svelte renders ModalShell and is opened by the share row menu item. It states who can watch and what is scrubbed, takes a lifetime (.share-select) and an optional label, then shows the one live link (.share-active, .share-row, .share-url) with a copy key ([data-share-copy], the base Button) and a revoke ([data-share-revoke], the base Button in the danger variant). Below the live link, a quiet line names how many links the install holds in all and offers "revoke all links" (.share-all, .share-all-text; two presses), which ends every minted link at once (the same route the plugin's purge uses); the notice line (.share-notice) reports a failed read or revoke. See docs/spectator-links.md.
