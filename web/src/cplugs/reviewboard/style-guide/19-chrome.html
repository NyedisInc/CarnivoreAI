review board plugin: the page, header, notes, layer, attachments and tray

review board page
The review board plugin's page (Joe (ccsliinc), Part 2, docs/reviewboard.md slice 4): a mockup under review, read only. It is the pop-out page's reviewboard tab, shown in the center screen as a page row or in a tab of its own. web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/BoardScreen.svelte (.rb) owns it and copies the flow chat view and the ledger chrome: a title strip (.rb-strip, fixed height, a hairline under it via box-shadow) with the board's title in mono (.rb-name), whose turn it is (.rb-state, small tracked capitals, --oc-coral when a round was not delivered) and icons only (.rb-tools, the base quiet IconButton: its reserved 1px hairline lights with the shared focus rule; a thumb target on a touch screen). The ONE accent-colored label is the pop-out title row's "review" (--oc-orange); the strip adds none. HistoryBar.svelte (.rb-history, .rb-track) is the board's spine: a revision is a small square carrying its number (the base small Button, the one on screen outlined in the confirm token --oc-orange; the dashed r1 to r12 chip past 20 revisions), a round Joe sent is a diamond between them (.rb-diamond inside a base quiet IconButton, a rotated hairline square, --oc-coral when undelivered), and .rb-count says r3 of 3. The pages of one revision are .rb-views, a base Segmented picker, with .rb-back (a base small Button, back to current) at the right. BoardFrame.svelte is the mockup: .rb-stage is the room, .rb-box sizes it (.rb-box--capped holds it to a width, centered: BOARD_MAX_WIDTH, 760px, on a layer board always, and on a canvas board the max_width its agent asked for; a capped box is laid out at that width and never scaled; the strip shows a width key on every capped board (titled full width / set width), an IconButton showing expand or shrink, to switch to full width), .rb-frame is the iframe, always sandbox="allow-scripts" and NEVER allow-same-origin, on the light Canvas ground like the artifact frame (an agent's page is not themed by the app). actual shows the design width inside a scroller, fit scales it down by a CSS transform. Under it .rb-readout says what the reader pointed at (.rb-label, the small tracked caption), the theme warning (.rb-chip in --oc-yellow) and the revision's summary. BoardPicker.svelte is the list shown when the address names no board: a base List of ListRows, each holding one whole-row base button (.rb-row-line, .rb-row-title, .rb-row-meta), no cards. Notes (.rb-note, .rb-note--error in --oc-coral). Tokens only; focus is the shared rule. A live board (issue #1725) adds a live state word in .rb-state, a draft mark at the end of the history track, and LiveBar.svelte: a quiet row of IconButton keys (bookmark, end) that opens one inline form (.rb-live-form, a base Field name box plus base Buttons go and cancel). InlineLayer.svelte draws the tray's prompts right under their anchored row over the page: .rb-inline is the layer, .rb-inline-block one block (hairline, dark fill, no card).
review board header controls
The board has NO sidebar (issue #1760, docs/reviewboard.md, "Payload pages"): its controls live in the title strip (.rb-strip, which wraps onto a second row on a narrow board) and the page frame takes the full width under it. Styled in web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/header.css. They are OUTSIDE the sandboxed frame, so the page can only supply its state and never press a key. ThemePicker.svelte is the base Select: it repaints only the page frame in another theme. For a revision that declares payload_mode, PayloadControls.svelte (.rb-controls) adds the save disk (the notepad widget's own EditorControls disk: --oc-yellow while the state is unsaved or saving, --oc-green once saved, --oc-coral with a short word when the write was refused; pressing it saves now), the save stepper (.rb-saves: older and newer IconButton keys around .rb-position, the .rb-label reading save 3 of 5 or draft), a note key (an IconButton, accent while a note waits) opening the notes panel below, and Send (a base small Button: confirm once there is a state to send, secondary before). PayloadNotices.svelte (.rb-notices) is the one quiet row under the strip: the confirm line after a send (.rb-confirm, .rb-warn) and the note of the save on show. Tokens only; focus is the shared rule; 40px keys on a touch screen.
review board notes panel
Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-14, issue #1788: "notes field bigger and a md editor with overlay and hints, it can sit up in the top right corner". NotesPanel.svelte (.rb-notes, styled in web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/header.css) is opened by the header's note key, a payload board's or the one beside a prompt board's prompts key, and is PINNED TOP RIGHT over the board body: a 1px --oc-line-frame hairline on --oc-ground-page, small square corners, no shadow (a frame of hairlines, as the tray's cards over the board). Its title strip (.rb-notes-head) copies .rb-strip: the titlerow ground, a hairline under it by box-shadow, the quiet .rb-label caption (.rb-notes-title, no second accent), a counter that shows only near the 8000 character cap (.rb-notes-count, --oc-text-muted, --oc-coral at the cap) and the close IconButton. The body (.rb-notes-body, inside .rb-notes-inner) is the one markdown editor of the cells section with its toggle and hints key, drawn framed so the text box shows its bounds and lights its own hairline on focus. It opens at 440 by 380, takes resize: both with its grip at the bottom left (the corner that moves, since the right edge is pinned), stays within the room and the viewport, and remembers its size per device; below 700px it is a bottom sheet across the body with no grip. A payload board's words are kept in this browser and follow the note (a send or an emptied note forgets them); a prompt board's live in its server draft. Escape closes it.
review board questions layer
Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-15, issue #1832: a questions layer is drawn INSIDE the page frame by plain JavaScript, not by the bundle, because the frame loads no app stylesheet (docs/reviewboard.md, "Layers"; the exception to "all new UI goes in web/" is in docs/DECISIONS.md). Its look is copied from this guide, not invented: src/cplugs/reviewboard/frame/layer.css restates the title strip (the titlerow ground, a hairline by box-shadow, ONE accent-colored uppercase label), the hairline-separated items, mono type, square 4px corners, quiet outlined controls with the picked one in the confirm outline (the accent border on a transparent fill, tinted on hover, as .modal-btn-confirm), the small tracked note key, and the shared focus rule (the control's own border a little brighter, no outline). Every color is an --oc-* token with a system-color fallback for the instant before the board's theme message arrives, so the theme picker repaints it. Its classes carry the rbl- prefix so an agent page's own stylesheet cannot hit them by accident, and none is registered in the registry because that stylesheet is a plugin file served into the sandbox, not a bundle stylesheet.
review board attachments key
Joe (ccsliinc), 2026-10-14, issue #1788: "a way for you to attach items to a board, paperclip icon". AttachmentsKey.svelte is the paperclip icon on an IconButton key in the title strip (inside .rb-clipwrap) with the file count in the accent (.rb-clip-count, as the prompts key's .rb-fold-count). A press opens a small fold under it (.rb-clippop, a free-floating popup, so it wears the one popup look, popup-focus, and declares no border, ground or shadow of its own), headed by the quiet .rb-label (.rb-clippop-head, a hairline under it), then one base ListRow per file, newest first (no cards): the name in mono, its size, date and revision (.rb-clip-error in --oc-coral when a read failed; .rb-clip-offline when the file has no route), and two IconButton keys, open in a new tab (external) and download (import), both reading the bytes with the app's own login. An empty or failed list is one quiet line (.rb-note-text, .rb-note). Below 700px the fold is a sheet across the screen; 40px keys on a touch screen. The list is read again on every boards push.
review board tray
Joe's review loop (Joe (ccsliinc), Part 2, docs/reviewboard.md slice 5): where he ticks, checks, writes notes and sends on a board that still asks prompts. Tray.svelte (.rb-fold, styled in web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/tray.css) is a FOLD-DOWN under the header, closed until the header's prompts key (a base Button with the count still unanswered, .rb-fold-count) opens it: it takes at most 55% of the room and scrolls inside itself (.rb-fold-body), with the sticky footer .rb-fold-foot: the clear key (a danger IconButton), the unanswered count (.rb-count) and send (a confirm icon Button). There is no column and no splitter: the mockup frame has the full width. PromptRow.svelte (.rb-prompt, a hairline between prompts, no cards) draws a choice or multi as option pills (.rb-pills of base small Buttons; the chosen one outlined in --oc-orange, the calm confirm token), a check with the shared Check, a question as a base multiline Field. The ghost line (.rb-ghost) shows an earlier answer to the same prompt id with a copy key and is NEVER pre-ticked. .rb-changed is what changed, .rb-notice the "landed while you wrote" notice (--oc-yellow), .rb-confirm the one line after a send (--oc-green, --oc-coral through .rb-warn when it was not delivered), the answered-round link a quiet base Button, .rb-anchors the pinned-note chips and fields, .rb-note-text small secondary text. RoundView.svelte and RelinkPicker.svelte are base Cards laid over the tray (.rb-over, .rb-sent, .rb-sent-note, .rb-actions): a round as sent, with resend and link to task when it was not delivered, and the board's task list as base list rows. .rb-body and .rb-main are the board's layout shell. On the task's thread a round draws as TaskManagerRoundCard.svelte (.hdrc, .hdrc-text, .hdrc-title, .hdrc-line, .hdrc-open), the sibling of the artifact card, with an icon that opens the board at that revision. Tokens only; the base gives every control its thumb target on a touch screen.
