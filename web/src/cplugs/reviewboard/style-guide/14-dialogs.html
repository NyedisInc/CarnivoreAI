public review links plugin: the share window

share a review board publicly
web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/public/ShareLinkModal.svelte renders ModalShell in the text-prompt-modal look and is opened by the board header's share key (web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/public/ShareLinkKey.svelte, an IconButton with the globe icon, contributed through web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/board-header-keys.ts so it exists only while the plugin is on). Two base RadioGroups pick how long the link lasts and which revision it shows, the base Button in the confirm variant makes it, and the whole link then shows ONCE in a base Field (.pbs-once) with a copy key and a warning line (.pbs-warn). Below, the board's links are a base List of rows: id, mode, end, a state Tag, and a danger IconButton to remove a live one; .pbs-label captions the list, .pbs-explain and .pbs-none are the quiet lines, a quiet danger Button "remove all" under the list opens the shared ConfirmPopup, .pbs-remove keeps a row's keys (copy, remove) one row high, .pbs-actions lays the buttons out and .pbs-error prints a refusal in the destructive color. See docs/reviewboard-public-links.md.
what visitors sent, and forward to claude
web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/public/VisitorsModal.svelte renders ModalShell in the same text-prompt-modal look and is opened by a second board header key (web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/public/VisitorsKey.svelte, an IconButton with the users icon, through the same seam). Both keys sit in the board's title strip with its other icons (.rb-tools). The visitors key wears the live count of responses not yet forwarded (.pbs-vcount, the accent, restated from the paperclip key's .rb-clip-count), and the window re-reads on every visitors push. Each response is a block (.pbs-vrow, hairlines between them inside .pbs-vlist, no cards): a head line (.pbs-vhead) with who sent it, always marked unverified (.pbs-vwho), its kind, revision and time (.pbs-vmeta) and a state Tag (new or forwarded), then the visitor's words as wrapped plain text (.pbs-vline), never markup. Each block's head ends in a danger IconButton (trash, in .pbs-remove) that removes it. One confirm Button, "forward to claude", is the owner's only way to send them on; a quiet danger Button "remove forwarded" beside it opens the shared ConfirmPopup; "read again" and "close" sit in the footer. .pbs-vmodal sets the width.
the visitor's answers and comment (public page)
web/src/cplugs/reviewboard/public/public/VisitorForm.svelte on the public page wears the base field and button looks (data-field, data-btn="confirm") laid out by public.css: .pbf sections, a question per .pbf-prompt with its choices in .pbf-options (each the base pick markup, .oc-pick, written out), text boxes as .pbf-text, the spot picker in .pbf-about, and the send keys in .pbf-actions beside one quiet result line (.pbf-said).
answers as u revisions (issue #2696 slice 5)
The share window adds a third base RadioGroup, what visitors can do (can answer, the default, or read only). On the public page the title strip gains the autosave word (.pb-save, muted, --oc-coral only when a save was refused) and, when the owner's next revision is cleared, the newer version key (.pb-newer: the quiet base button outlined in --oc-cyan, the notice token). A read only link draws no control. The visitors window opens with who answered (.pbs-ulist, one full-width base small Button per visitor and revision): the u label as a badge in --oc-cyan (.pbs-ubadge, with .pbs-here, the small "viewing now" word in the live color, beside it while that visitor is on the page, the same token the board's history bar gives a u chip), the revision and last edit (.pbs-vmeta) and a Tag, sent or not sent; a press opens that u revision on the board page.
