web/src/cplugs/crate/crate-dialog.css: what the export and restore dialogs share, so neither restates it. .crt-modal on the overlay widens the text-prompt-modal recipe to 600px, stacks it no taller than the window, scrolls only the body and pins the footer under a hairline so the buttons never move; a phone gets the whole screen. .crt-modal--fill (the restore form) makes the dialog a fixed frame as tall as the window allows and gives the terminal box whatever the summary and the form leave, so the box is whole on screen and nothing under it moves. These chassis rules are unlayered, as .analytics-modal's are, because the legacy sheet they widen is. The form is a column on the space steps (.crt-form, its fields the base Field, its ticks the base Checkbox in .crt-ticks, two columns once each has 16rem); the done screen is .crt-plan, its last button .crt-plan__act. The summary strip .crt-sum is four aligned lines, a .crt-sum__key in tracked capitals and a one-line .crt-sum__val cut with an ellipsis (--bright for the crate's name, --unread dim before a plan answers). While a request with no progress of its own runs, .crt-measure shows a pulsing green square .crt-measure__dot, the sentence .crt-measure__sub and the seconds .crt-measure__figs; the pulse is still when motion is reduced or off.
TtyBox, the terminal box
web/src/cplugs/crate/TtyBox.svelte: the inset readout every list in the crate dialogs lives in, so a crate with hundreds of items scrolls inside the box and never stretches the dialog. .crt-tty is the field ground with a 1px hairline frame drawn as an inset shadow and small square corners; .crt-tty__bar is a short title row (.crt-tty__label at the left, .crt-tty__state at the right holding the wait row or a ready or blocked tag); .crt-tty__screen scrolls on its own at a fixed height so the box never jumps between idle, waiting and a plan, and --fit shrinks it to a short list, capped at the same height. Inside, .crt-tty__out is the column (--lines for the export plan's sentences), .crt-tty__idle the one dim sentence before a plan. A .crt-tty__note is a tag and a wrapping .crt-tty__text (--bad coral for a blocker, --dim for its detail). A .crt-tty__sec is one grid whose rows (.crt-tty__row) are its subgrid, so the columns line up down the whole list: the name (.crt-tty__name, takes the slack, ellipsis, full text as its title), the figure (.crt-tty__fig, dim, right, tabular digits; --left in a .crt-tty__sec--pair, which is two equal columns for from and to or what and why) and the outcome tag. A section's .crt-tty__head sticks to the top of the screen with its .crt-tty__count; a .crt-tty__line is a full-width line (--bright for the project's action); .crt-tty__act puts a small button in the tag column. Below 440px of box a row puts its name on a line of its own.
outcome tag
The outcome tag is the base Tag (.oc-tag): ok green (new, ready, written here), info cyan (grows the copy here), warn yellow (newer here, from elsewhere, a warning), danger coral (conflict, running, blocked), and neutral for already here, left out, held back. The tag says the outcome in a word; the full sentence sits in the row's title.
ImportDialog
web/src/cplugs/crate/ImportDialog.svelte: the "restore" dialog a recorded or found crate opens through openModal. Summary first: the text-prompt-modal head, then .crt-sum (the crate, its source, where it lands, the totals), then .crt-form: one "restore into" .oc-field (the crate's original folder; only the owner's typing or click changes it; a plain line under it and a quiet .modal-btn-secondary that takes the plan's suggested folder appear when the folder is unusable here), a password .oc-field for an encrypted crate's key, and a Check only for what means something for THIS plan. Then the terminal box with the preview, then the notes. The footer is a quiet .modal-btn-secondary cancel and the confirming .modal-btn-confirm, which reads preview until a plan that can be restored is on screen and restore after. Once the restore starts, JobProgress.svelte takes the body and the footer holds one quiet button.
RemoveDialog
web/src/cplugs/crate/RemoveDialog.svelte: the confirm a crate row's "remove" opens through openModal. The text-prompt-modal head, one plain sentence (the crate and its checksum file go to the Trash and can be put back), a .tm__sub line for a recorded crate (it stays in the list, marked removed), and a Check for the second folder's copy only when another copy exists (off by default). The footer is the restart picker's: a quiet .modal-btn-secondary cancel and the destructive .modal-btn-danger, in its own token and never the confirming one.
UndoDialog
web/src/cplugs/crate/UndoDialog.svelte: the confirm a restored row's "undo" opens through openModal. The text-prompt-modal head, one plain sentence saying what the undo removes (the database rows and files the restore wrote, the sessions and project it made) and that anything already here or changed since stays, then a TtyBox listing each row that stays with its reason. A blocker (a later restore, a running job, a step that cannot be undone cleanly) is a .tm__problem line and keeps the undo off. The footer is the restart picker's: a quiet .modal-btn-secondary cancel and the destructive .modal-btn-danger; after the undo the body says what was removed and one quiet button closes it.
ImportPreview
web/src/cplugs/crate/ImportPreview.svelte: the read-only plan on the box's screen, in the order an owner decides by: who wrote the crate, every blocker, every warning, then a section each for the project, the folder mapping, the conversations (name, how it resumes, outcome tag), the records that did something (table, counts, tag), the instructions held back, and what is never restored and why.
ImportResult
web/src/cplugs/crate/ImportResult.svelte: the restore's done screen, a .crt-plan that replaces the job's progress. A .tm__figs line says how many sessions came back asleep and in which project, the folder dim under it, then a fitted terminal box: the warnings, one row per session (its name, its state in the figure column, a small .oc-btn that wakes it through the app's own wake route), the boards kept beside ones already here, the folder the project's files went into, and what was not restored. The last control is an .oc-btn to the Projects screen.
ExportDialog
web/src/cplugs/crate/ExportDialog.svelte: the crates plugin's "export this project" dialog, opened through openModal from a project's crates group. The text-prompt-modal recipe and head: one .modal-description sentence, then .crt-form (a base Field label, a base RadioGroup for own or share copy, base Checkboxes for encrypt and the rest), and after "preview" the plan under a hairline (.crt-plan: the counts in .tm__figs, each blocker in .tm__problem with the server's own words dim under it, warnings dim). The footer is a quiet .modal-btn-secondary cancel and the confirming .modal-btn-confirm, which reads preview (previewing while it runs, with a wait row .crt-measure under the form: a pulsing green square .crt-measure__dot, the sentence and the seconds so far, still when motion is reduced) until a plan that can be written is on screen and export after; a changed choice drops the plan. Once the export starts, JobProgress.svelte takes the body and the footer holds one quiet button.
