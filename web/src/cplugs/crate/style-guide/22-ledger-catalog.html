crate plugin: the crates group on a project page

The crates plugin's CRATES group (web/src/cplugs/crate/ProjectCrates.svelte) is the second group of its kind: an action line (.crt-pacts) holding the confirming "export this project" button, then one base list row per crate that holds the project or one of its sessions (name, then the age at the right), with a line under it holding the scope and copies as base tags and a line of quiet buttons (verify, restore, download and a coral remove; .crt-pacts__line wraps them), "show all" and a started verify's progress under the rows (.crt-pjob). It never folds itself when empty, because its action lives inside it.
