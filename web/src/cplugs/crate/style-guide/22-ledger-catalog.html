crate plugin: the crates group on a project page

The crates plugin's CRATES group (web/src/cplugs/crate/ProjectCrates.svelte) is the second group of its kind: an action line (.crt-pacts) holding the confirming outline "export this project", then one .crt-prow per crate that holds the project or one of its sessions, the closed review row's twin on .ph-scols (its parts .crt-prow__lead, .crt-prow__name, .crt-prow__meta with the scope and copies as .ph-tags, .crt-prow__when, .crt-prow__act holding a quiet verify, and .crt-prow__pad for the kebab column on a phone), "show all" (.crt-pmore) and a started verify's progress under the rows (.crt-pjob). It never folds itself when empty, because its action lives inside it.
